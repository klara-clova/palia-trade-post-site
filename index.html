How it works

Three steps to your next trade

No spreadsheets, no re-posting your list every week. Tell the bot once, and it keeps watching.

1

Post your list

Run /trade and pick up to 5 items you want and 5 you're offering, straight from autocomplete — no typos, no guessing names. Run it again anytime to add more, nothing gets overwritten.

2

Get matched instantly

The moment there's a real two-way match — you have what they want, and they have what you want — you're both pinged in a private thread only the two of you can see, with images of exactly what's changing hands.

3

Trade & close it out

Sort the trade out in-game, then tap Mark as completed — the items clear from both your lists, and the thread and your original posts disappear automatically.

Commands

Everything, right in Discord

/trade

Post up to 5 items you want and 5 you're offering — matching runs automatically, and you can run it again anytime to add more.

/mylist

See your current offers and wants, with images, and clear anything out with a tap.

/browse

Flip through the full catalog as a visual grid, with instant search.

/find

Pick a category, then jump straight to an item and see its image right away.

/clearlist

Wipe your entire want and offer list in one go — handy for starting fresh.
