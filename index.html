How it works

Three steps to your next trade

No spreadsheets, no re-posting your list every week. Tell the bot once, and it keeps watching.

1

Post your list

Run /trade and pick up to 3 items you want and 3 you're offering, straight from autocomplete — no typos, no guessing names.

2

Get matched instantly

The moment someone else's list lines up with yours, you're both pinged in a new thread — with images of exactly what's changing hands.

3

Trade & close it out

Sort the trade out in-game, then tap Mark as completed — the items clear from both your lists and the thread wraps up.
